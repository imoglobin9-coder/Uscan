Privacy

uscan reads text from the reports you upload, lets you correct it, and can combine everything into one PDF. This page explains what happens to your files.

No account

There is nothing to sign up for, and uscan does not ask for your name or email. Your files live in a private workspace tied to a random cookie in this browser. Only that browser can open it.

What is stored, and for how long

  • The files you upload, the text read from them, page images, and any PDFs you generate.
  • They are deleted automatically {{TTL}} after you last used uscan.
  • You can delete everything immediately with Delete my data at the top of the page. Deleting a single report removes it too.

Who can see your files

Other visitors cannot. The person who runs this server can technically reach files stored on it, and backups or logs kept by the hosting provider are outside uscan's control. Files are not encrypted on the server, so please do not upload anything you would not be comfortable sending to a website.

Cookies

One cookie (uscan_ws) keeps your workspace connected to your browser. It is strictly necessary for the site to work. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and uscan itself does not use analytics. If you delete the cookie you lose access to your workspace, and its files are removed automatically after the time above.

Logs

uscan does not keep an access log of who visited or what they opened. Error messages may be written to the server log. Your network address is used briefly in memory to limit abuse and is not stored. The hosting provider may keep its own logs.
